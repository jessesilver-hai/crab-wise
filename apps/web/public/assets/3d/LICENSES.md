# 3D Asset Licenses

All assets under `assets/3d/` are CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication). No
attribution is required, but crediting Kay Lousberg (KayKit) is appreciated
by the author. No CC-BY, CC-BY-SA, GPL, or restricted-license assets are
included.

CC0 1.0: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

All files were converted/optimized from the original glTF releases:
static meshes were repacked as .glb with the shared texture atlas embedded
and quantized (`KHR_mesh_quantization`); rigged characters additionally had
unused animation clips stripped and animation curves resampled. Three.js
`GLTFLoader` handles `KHR_mesh_quantization` natively — no extra decoder
needed (no Draco/meshopt used).
